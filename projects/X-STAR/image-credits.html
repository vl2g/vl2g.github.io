Credits for dataset and retrieval examples

The public site displays one Telugu signboard dataset example and five retrieval rows. These records cover all 26 audited photographs, including examples not currently displayed: 24 from BSTD and two from RCTW-17. The table below credits the BSTD photographs; the RCTW-17 reuse evidence is recorded separately below. Source-file hashes and sizes have been checked against Wikimedia Commons. Credits were checked on 13 November 2026. The images keep their own licenses, separate from the MIT website-code license.
